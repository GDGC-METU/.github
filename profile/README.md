# 🚀 GDG on Campus METU — Production Team

Welcome to the official **Production Team** organization of **GDG on Campus METU**! We build real-world software, robotics, game development, and engineering solutions.

---

## 🎯 Our Goal
> **"Making real products and innovation"**

Our mission is to foster innovation, transition from theory to execution, and construct impactful systems.

---

## 🛠️ Projects & Teams

Our teams are organized across hardware, software, perception, and interactive development:

- **Ready-Made Vehicles Team:** Autonomous control, perception, and software integration on existing platforms.
- **Original Vehicle Development Team:** Custom platform design, hardware integration, and specialized software.
- **Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAV) Team:** Flight control, vision pipelines, and aerial platform development.
- **Game Dev Team:** Interactive mechanics, 2D/3D graphics, game engines, and immersive experiences.
- **Others:** Interdisciplinary initiatives, experimental concepts, and creative software projects.
